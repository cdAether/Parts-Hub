Chasing AW11 parts? Add them to the next box.

Parts for the first-generation MR2 are easier to find in New Zealand than here. Requests from NSW owners are combined into one shipment so the freight is shared.

  1. Tell us about your car and the parts you need.
  2. Your list is checked with the supplier in NZ, who confirms what he has, its condition and a price.
  3. You get photos and parts prices, and nothing is ordered until you agree. Your share of the freight is confirmed once the box is packed and weighed.
